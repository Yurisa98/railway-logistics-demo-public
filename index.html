Calculation details

Daily stock balance and staffing formulas used in the latest run.

Inventory ledger

Opening stock + arrivals/production − departures = closing stock. Peak is the highest stock seen during the day.

Staffing calculation

Shift need = round up (cycles × hands-on minutes ÷ available minutes per worker per shift) × crew size. Backlog need uses that process's backlog cycles and deadline hours, with available time capped at one shift per worker.
